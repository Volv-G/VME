Privacy

Courtside VB, VME and the VME streaming app · updated November 2026

Courtside VB is a private site where a volleyball club shares match videos, scores and stats with its players' families and coaches. VME is the club's own software that edits those videos and publishes them, and the VME streaming app broadcasts matches live. This page says what they keep about you and what they do with it.

What the site keeps

  • Your email address, which is how you sign in.
  • Your request to join a team: the team, whether you are a coach or which player's family, any note you add, and the club's decision.
  • Which scores, stats and matches you have chosen to see, so your devices agree and nothing is spoiled.
  • Changes you suggest to a player's name or photo.

Only the club's administrators see who has asked to join. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking. Videos play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, which sets no cookies until you press play.

Emails

When you ask to join a team, the administrators get an email saying so; when your request is approved, you get one. When a match starts streaming, the club may email its team mailing list. These are the only emails sent.

Google account access

VME and the streaming app sign in to the club's own Google account, not yours. They use it to:

  • YouTube: upload match videos and run live broadcasts on the club's channel, and add them to its playlists.
  • Gmail (send only): send the emails described above. They cannot read, search or delete any email.

The sign-in is kept on the club's computer and phone and is not shared with anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

Ask the club's site administrator to remove you from a team. That deletes your membership; your other records go with it on request.
